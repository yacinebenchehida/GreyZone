# Fin-whale skin artwork

`fin-whale.webp` is generated illustrative artwork for the fin-whale option, with genuine transparency. It was created with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP without changing the artwork. The original PNG remains in the task workspace.

Prompt: A single realistic fin whale (Balaenoptera physalus), strict orthographic dorsal view, head right and flukes left, on transparent background. Long slender body, flattened V-shaped rostrum, short swept pectorals, modest falcate dorsal fin at about two-thirds body length, charcoal brown-grey skin, subdued shoulder chevron, pale right lower-jaw edge, paired blowholes and tiny eyes. Broad triangular notched flukes. Natural skin detail and soft physical lighting. No humpback tubercles, long white flippers, water, foam, spout, labels or background.

The body texture is bent along the numerical drawing spine; it is decorative and does not encode population state. Genotype dots remain a separate live layer. A separate procedural layer supplies the occasional columnar blow, droplets and surface ripple. The image loads only when the whale renderer is requested.

Anatomical references: NOAA Fisheries fin-whale species description (https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/species/fin-whale) and NOAA marine-mammal identification guide (https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/35695/noaa_35695_DS1.pdf). Generated artwork is not a documentary photograph.
